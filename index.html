🏆 Matchup Breakdowns

Blowout

No Niners No Problems 150.64 vs Quant 83.84

Phil dropped the week's high score, which is impressive. It's more impressive that he did it with Drake Maye's 39.46 points sitting on his bench. He started Jared Goff (28.94) instead, so the optimal lineup would have cleared 160. Phil left a whole tight end's worth of points on the pine and still won by 66.80, the biggest blowout of the week. Jaylen Warren (33.80), Christian Watson (29.30), and Malik Nabers (23.60) did the heavy lifting.

Dion, meanwhile, rolled out Justin Herbert (11.24) while Malik Willis put up 20.88 on the bench. Justin Jefferson added 15.80 of decorative bench points. None of it would have mattered. Quant falls to 1-4, and the model is recommending a rebuild.

Mercy rule

Cyclospora 143.78 vs Hard Count Laura 93.50

Alex rode Jahmyr Gibbs (35.20, best starter in the league this week) and Jordan Love (25.28) to a comfortable 50-point win. Don't hand out the Coach of the Year award just yet, though. Alex started Rome Odunze in the flex for 3.50 points while MarShawn Lloyd put up 16.20 on the bench. It's easy to look smart when your opponent is trying even harder to lose.

Laura is now 0-5. She started Joe Burrow (24.60) over Jayden Daniels (32.26). She also started Jordan Addison, who scored a crisp, clean 0.00, while Pat Freiermuth put up 19.00 on her bench. The team name is starting to feel less like a joke and more like a diagnosis.

Bucky ball

Chingados Unidos 132.36 vs Mom 116.88

Bucky Irving went off for 30.50 out of the flex, and John's whole lineup showed up. Sarah got a surprise 25.78 from Jacoby Brissett and 22.80 from Mike Evans. Then Carnell Tate contributed 1.10 points, which is roughly what you get for standing near a football. Mom drops to 2-3, and John stays hot at 4-1.

Boring works

Stale 111.72 vs I… am no longer sure 91.90

Owen ran the boring, effective formula: Stafford (27.32), James Cook (22.30), Derrick Henry (20.50).

Dara got 31.50 from Josh Allen in an overtime thriller. She also got 1.90 from CeeDee Lamb and 3.80 from Jaylen Waddle, with 64.64 points sitting on her bench. The good news: run the optimal lineup and she still loses by 1.42. The team name has never been more accurate.

Kicker szn

Tater Surprise 109.84 vs Portland Puddle Boys 101.44

After eating last week's 64-point blowout, the Commish bounced back thanks to… kicker Spencer Shrader with 21.00 points. Truly the most Travis way to win a football game. Parker Washington (27.40) and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (20.30) helped too, but we all know who the real MVP was.

Tyler got 29.20 from Puka Nacua but started Stefon Diggs in the flex for a goose egg. Portland's two-game win streak washes away.

Heartbreaker

The White Bucket 106.84 vs Mini Me 100.50

Galen stays in first place, mostly thanks to Isaiah Likely (26.90) and Ka'imi Fairbairn (17.00). This came during a weekend in which Galen made three roster moves before kickoff. He dropped Tyreek Hill, picked up and then cut Tre' Harris within about six hours, and grabbed Bryce Young. The man treats the waiver wire like a group chat. He leads the league with 11 moves, and somehow it's working.

George, I'm so sorry. You lost by 6.34. Jameson Williams scored 17.70 on your bench while Deebo Samuel scored 1.50 in your lineup. That one swap flips the result. Mini Me falls to 3-2, and George will be thinking about this until at least Thursday.
